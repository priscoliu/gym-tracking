Progression Rules

  • Log every set. All sets at top of rep range → add 2.5kg next session.
  • Top sets: add 2.5kg at 6 clean reps.
  • Last isolation set of curls/laterals: lengthened partials after failure.
  • Any lift flat 4+ weeks: check session order first, then swap.
  • Deload every 6 weeks: one week at 60% load.
